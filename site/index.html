How money stays safe

The problem

Formally included. Functionally excluded.

TalkBack, Android’s screen reader, only works when an app is built for it. Many Indian UPI and shopping apps use unlabeled icons, gesture menus and CAPTCHAs with no audio option. So blind users ask a nephew to send money for them.

    Safety gate
    
      
    The AI suggests. Plain code decides.
    
      
    Every tap Saathi wants to make goes through a safety check first. That check is ordinary, tested code, not an AI guess. It can let the tap through, hold it until you say yes, or stop it.
    

      
    
        
    Hard line
    Never touches your PIN
    On a PIN, password or OTP screen, Saathi stops, turns TalkBack back on and says “अपना UPI PIN डालिए”.
    
        
    Hard line
    Never reads back a guess
    The name and amount it reads to you must appear word for word on the screen. If they don’t, it asks you instead.
    
        
    Hard line
    Unclear means cancel
    If it doesn’t hear a clear “haan”, it asks once more. Still unclear? It cancels.

    Hold SPACE or press ESC at any moment and Saathi stops mid-step. Whenever it stops, finishes or crashes, TalkBack is switched back on, so you’re never left without your screen reader.
    
    
    
  
    

  
  
    
    
    
      
    How it works
    
      
    One step at a time, checked every time.
    
      
    For each command, Saathi repeats the same four steps until the job is done, up to 25 steps. If three steps in a row don’t change the screen, it stops and tells you.
    
      
    
        
    Look
    Reads every button and label on the phone’s screen. Takes a screenshot only when labels are missing.
    device.observe()
    
        
    Decide
    An AI model picks exactly one next action and a short Hindi line to say.
    {"type": "tap", "index": 4}
    
        
    Check
    The safety gate allows it, holds it for your yes, or stops it.
    safety.check()
    
        
    Act
    Taps, types or scrolls, then confirms the screen actually changed.
    wait_for_change()

    What you can ask
    
      
    Four everyday jobs, done well.
    
      
    We picked the tasks blind users most often hand to someone else, and focused on making them reliable rather than adding more apps.
    
      
    
        
    
          Core
          
    WhatsApp: read and reply
    
          
    मम्मी का last message पढ़ो
    
          
    “Read Mummy’s last message.” Then: “Reply: main 7 baje aaunga.”
    
          
    Before sending, Saathi reads back who it’s going to and the exact words.
    
        
    
        
    
          Core
          
    Google Pay: send money
    
          
    राहुल को एक रुपया भेजो
    
          
    “Send Rahul one rupee.” If there are two Rahuls, it asks which one.
    
          
    Reads back the name, UPI ID and amount, then hands over for your PIN.
    
        
    
        
    
          Next
          
    Blinkit: order groceries
    
          
    Blinkit से अमूल दूध 1 लीटर मंगाओ
    
          
    “Order 1 litre of Amul milk from Blinkit.”
    
          
    Reads back the items and the total before placing the order.
    
        
    
        
    
          Next
          
    Any app: what’s on screen?
    
          
    स्क्रीन पे क्या है?

    One sentence about the screen, plus the top three things you can do there.
    
        
    
      
    
    
    
  
    

  
  
    
    
    
      
    Measured, not promised
    
      
    We test every flow on a real phone.
    
      
    An automated test runs each task ten times on a real phone. It never places real orders, and test payments are capped at ₹10 to our own accounts. The striped bars are our targets. Measured results replace them when the test run finishes.
